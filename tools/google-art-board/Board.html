Deck builder

Click a card to add it, and press − to take one out. A deck is a Main Deck of exactly 30 creatures and spells plus a Fusion Deck of up to 10 fusions, with at most 3 copies of any card. Fusions go to the Fusion Deck on their own. The deck panel checks every rule as you go, and warns you about fusions your creatures can't make. Your deck stays in this browser.
