# Fusion Fuckery: patch notes

Every change to the rules and the cards, newest first, from the first ruleset to now. The rules notes are written by
hand; the **card changes** under each version are worked out from the saved card list, so every new, removed,
renamed or reworded card is in there (open "cards reworded" to see the old and new text). Before the card editor
(2026-10-06) the cards lived in a spreadsheet with its own versions, card lists v1 to v6; those are at the end, after
the rulesets, because their numbers don't line up with the ruleset numbers.

## 2026-11-28

**Rules**
- **Speed has no minimum or maximum.** Printed Speed still runs 1–8, but effects can take it to 0, below 0, or above 8. (You're FIRED!'s Speed loss used to stop at 1.)

No card changes.

## 2026-11-27: removal, small creatures, plush rework

**Rules**
- **Face-down spells:** in your Main Phase you can set a Spell, Instant Spell or Continuous Spell face down in an empty spell zone (not a Field Spell). From your opponent's next turn on, flip it and play it whenever you could play an Instant, on their turn too. It counts as played when it's flipped. Pipebomb can destroy it while it's face down.
- **No more attacker's advantage:** the attacker no longer gets +1 Speed. On a speed tie the **attacker strikes first** (it was a coin flip). Twisted Dimensions still reverses Speed, and a tie still goes to the attacker.
- **Creatures can attack the turn they arrive.** Only fusions wait a turn (they arrive winded), and creatures that come back winded from Unfuse.
- **Unfuse materializes** the fusion splices, so their "when materialized" effects trigger.
- **New keywords:** **Underdog** (this creature deals 2 more combat damage to fusions) and **Execute** (when this creature deals combat damage to a creature, if that creature has half its max HP or less left, destroy it).
- **Plushies:** a plushy materialized with no Claw Machine on the field (anyone's) is destroyed, so its death effect triggers. Before, you couldn't materialize one without your Claw Machine.
- **Speed loss** (You're FIRED!) is permanent, but Speed never goes below 1.
- All saved decks, the starter decks included, were removed.

**Cards**
- **Removal:** Ritual Pit is back from cold storage as a Spell: destroy a creature, its controller draws 1 card. Goblin General is now **Hostile Takeover**: once per turn, pay gold equal to a creature's current HP to destroy it (its old effects are gone). Gob-omber's blast deals double damage to creatures that cost 6 or more flux and to fusions.
- **The Flock is now The Feeble Fusion:** 2 or 3 of your creatures of any flux cost whose flux adds up to 7 or more; 6/16, Speed 4, with Execute.
- **Underdog** on Acolyte Adam, Vein Ripper, Penny Stock Goblin, Velociraptor, Wild Magic Wizard and Plush Goblin.
- **Boss's Orders is now You're FIRED!:** 1 damage to a creature, and it loses 2 Speed.
- **Déjà Vu** works once per game. **Shotgun Wedding** needs at least one fusion splice from your field.
- **Plush rework:** Claw Machine now rolls a d6 once per turn, and on a 6 you may materialize a Fluffy creature from your Waste Repository. Arcade Kid only searches for the Claw Machine. Plush Salem: when destroyed, draw 1 then discard 1. Plush Sanctified Knight: when it dies, 1 damage to the creatures next to it. Plush T-Rex: when it dies, 3 damage to the enemy creature in front of it (and it still returns a Fluffy creature to your hand at the end of your turn). Patchwork Plush gets +2 ATK and +2 HP for each Fluffy creature on the field and in your Waste Repository, and still costs 1 less for each one in your Waste Repository.
- **Salem and Plush Salem are Noble.** Chimera's card spells out that its 3 Beasts can cost any flux.

## 2026-11-26: balance patch

**Rules**
- **Radiance** only works the first time each turn a creature is restored past its max HP. Energy Drink can't trigger it.
- **Descend** costs half the flux, rounded up, and the creature arrives winded (it takes the hit without hitting back). Cherub and Acolyte Adam get Descend. Bellkeeper Monk and Sanctuary Bells still take 1 off, never below 0.
- **Fossil** works once per turn per card name. **Blood Price** is once per turn per card name.
- **"Once per turn"** is printed on the cards as "You can only use this effect of [card] once per turn" (it always counted per card name). One-shot spells say "You can only play 1 [card] per turn".
- **Melting Pot** takes over Ritual Pit's effect for any creature (banish a creature from your Waste Repository: your next fusion this turn may use a 3–4 flux creature). Only its owner can use it. Ritual Pit goes into cold storage.
- New stat-budget guideline: a 10 flux card should be about 8 times a 1 flux card.

**Cards**
- **Nerfs:** Indulgence Seller (HP equal to the gold, a fusion splice counts as 2 gold), Ergof The Dark Emperor (4 PHP for 5 HP or less, a splice for 8 HP or less), Renroc The Dark Marquis (3 PHP), Archaeologist (mills 2), Market Research (2 gold), Goblin Shuffle (no extra card), Jar of Materialism (draw 1, your opponent gains 4 PHP), Patchwork Plush (10 flux), Microwave and Shotgun Wedding (1 per turn).
- **Reworks:** Lord Lazarus (3/12, Speed 2, +3 ATK / +5 HP / +1 Speed per fusion splice, takes a Noble creature from your Waste Repository as a splice once per turn); Fallen Angel (no effect, 24 HP); Fossil Record (up to 2 Prehistoric creatures from your deck into your Waste Repository); Card Trick (a random card from their hand into their deck, they draw a new one); Shotgun Wedding (a temporary fusion that can attack straight away, not the player, destroyed at the end of your turn); Crack a pack (a new sealed pack: keep 1, banish the rest); Loaded Dice (choose the result, no side effects); Arcade Kid (counts Fluffy creatures in your Waste Repository too, and costs more for them); Goblin Pit Boss (rerolls as the die is rolled).
- **Smaller:** Ankylosaurus reduces any damage; Pocket Fireball can hit a player; Pipebomb negates as well; Overclock can't feed itself; Plush Catalogue can't find another Plush Catalogue; Unfuse lets the owner choose what goes to the Waste Repository; a Space Squid'd fusion's splices are yours too; Gabe 8/16, Judicator Angel 6/12; Beelzechud, Carrion The Dark Duchess and Nomed The Dark Viscount are Winged.
- Starters: Melting Pot replaces Ritual Pit in Fallen Grace and Bone Yard.

(The first 2026-11-27 card changes, You're FIRED!, Déjà Vu, Unfuse and Twisted Dimensions, were saved together with this patch, so they show up in the list below.)

## v4.5 (2026-10-27): fusion splices and Plushies

**Rules**
- A fusion **keeps the damage** its fusion splices had and **arrives winded**.
- **Two fusions can be fused**, and every card in the stack counts as a fusion splice.
- **Unfuse** splits the fusion's damage between the splices it returns (never below 1 HP) and keeps them winded.
- On a direct attack, winded creatures next to the attacker still hit back, last.
- Every fusion uses its fusion splices, by banishing one for an effect or by counting them.
- Creatures that cost 5+ flux (the ones that can fuse) get a gold flux badge.
- **New faction: Plushies** (Fluffy creatures and the Claw Machine), with a starter deck.

**Cards**
- Blood Ledger is a Continuous Spell (was a Field Spell). New: Boss's Orders, Melting Pot, Twisted Dimensions, Shotgun Wedding, and 14 Plushy cards. Arch Cleric, The Meat Cathedral and Seraph of Judgment toned down. Renames, flavour text and lore.

## Rulebook v3 and v4.2 (2026-10-19/20): gameplay v2

A new game on top of v2.1: lanes, Speed and paid fusing. (The rulebook was called v3 on 2026-10-19 and renamed v4.2 the same day; v4.2 also took in the fixes below.)

**Rules**
- **30 PHP**, no maximum at first (from v4.2 restoring can't take you above 30, gaining can). Running out of cards costs 5 PHP per draw. A draw if both hit 0.
- **Opening hand 5**; mulligan only if both players agree. **Draw 1** a turn. The second player gets **+1 flux** on their first turn.
- **Flux starts at 3**, +1 a turn, max 10. No more Meltdown. Spells are free. A discount below 0 gives you the difference (v4.2).
- **The play area:** **3 lanes** (one creature or fusion each), **3 spell zones** and **1 Field Spell zone** per player. Field Spells affect both sides; Continuous Spells affect yours (v4.2).
- **Turn:** Draw, Reactor, Start, Main 1 (materialize, spells, effects, one fusion), Battle, Main 2 (no materializing or fusing), End (discard to 8).
- **Lane combat, no blocking:** an attacker hits the creature in front of it or in a lane next to it; with nothing in front, it can hit the player, and enemy creatures in the lanes next to it hit back. **Speed** (1–4) decides who hits first instead of simultaneous damage; on a tie the attacker goes first; a creature destroyed by the first hit doesn't hit back. From v4.2 **the attacker gets +1 Speed**.
- **Fusing:** once per turn in Main Phase 1, **pay 2 flux**, two creatures that each cost **5 or more flux**, in lanes next to each other, meeting the fusion's recipe. Ways around the 2 flux: It's Free Fuse-Estate, Crimson Altar. Ritual Pit lets a 3–4 flux Unholy creature be a splice.
- **Fusions don't inherit** their splices' effects (v4.2; v3 still carried them over, as in v2.1). Only the fusion's own death effects trigger. Fusion materials are called **fusion splices**.
- **Keywords:** Radiance (from v4.2: restored HP past max becomes your choice of +ATK or +HP until your next turn), Blood Price (2 PHP per flux in v3, **3 PHP per flux** from v4.2), dice ATK (rolled on every attack and hit back).
- **Give vs restore HP** (v4.2): giving HP is permanent and raises max HP; restoring can't go past max HP.
- Golden rules: the card wins; your cards, your job; newest first. (v3 also had "can't beats can" and "do as much as you can"; v4.2 dropped them.)
- Card text names attributes (Holy, Unholy, Greedy…), not factions. A normal Spell has no type symbol.
- Dino, Mutant, Parasite, Bleed and Equipment cards go into cold storage.

## Rulebook v2.1: The Blocking Update (Quick Start)

**Rules**
- **Deck building:** max **3 copies** of any card (was 6 of a creature and 3 of a spell). Fusion Deck up to 10, face up.
- **Setup:** shuffle and let your opponent cut; the coin-flip winner picks who goes first; **draw 6**, with one reshuffle-and-redraw.
- **Flux:** creatures cost flux; spells and fusions are free. Max flux starts at 1, +1 a turn, cap 10. **Meltdown:** from your 13th turn you have unlimited flux.
- **Turn:** Draw 2, Reactor, Start ("survive" effects first), **Main Phase 1** (materialize, spells, effects, up to 1 fusion), Battle, Main Phase 2, End (Bleeding damage, then discard down to 8). The Standby and separate Materialize/Fusion phases are gone.
- **Spells:** Spell (your Main Phases), Instant Spell (your Main Phases or in response), Continuous Spell, Field Spell (**only one in play on the whole table**; a new one kicks out the old one).
- **Blocking:** you declare attackers only; your opponent declares defenders (any number, several can gang up on one attacker); damage is simultaneous; an unblocked attacker hits PHP; an attacker that's blocked never hits PHP and splits its damage between its defenders.
- **Fusion:** once per turn in Main Phase 1; keeps the splices' effects, not their stats; max 2 fusions; if any splice came into play this turn, the fusion can't attack.
- **Golden rules:** the card wins; can't beats can; do as much as you can; your cards, your job; 5 PHP per card you can't draw.

## Ruleset v5

**Rules**
- **Positioning matters:** creatures can't move once materialized.
- **Turn:** Start Phase ("survive" effects go first), **Standby Phase**, Materialize Phase, Standby Phase 2, Fusion Phase, Battle Phase (skipped on the first turn of the game), Standby Phase 3, End Phase. Instant windows in battle are for the opponent.
- If you forget a positive effect, it doesn't resolve: you're responsible for triggering them.
- **Fusions keep their splices' effects** (not their stats); the same effect twice only counts once.
- **Warm vs cold fusion:** if every splice has been on the field for more than a turn, the fusion can attack straight away; if one or more just arrived, it can't.
- The Mutant "only forced to mutate once per turn" rule is gone.

## Ruleset v4

**Rules**
- A **setup** section: exactly 30 cards, 6-card opening hand, a coin flip for who goes first; the first player skips their draw and can't attack on their first turn.
- New story intro: Angels, Blood Demons, Mutants, Parasites, dinosaurs and Gregg.

## Ruleset v3

**Rules**
- **HP definitions:** current HP, natural HP (printed), max HP (natural + permanent gains). Restoring can't go above max HP; permanently gaining HP raises both current and max.
- Invested gold can't be taken back out of a card.

## Ruleset v2: Flux

**Rules**
- **Flux:** creatures cost flux. Max flux starts at 1 and goes up by 1 each turn in the new **Reactor Phase**, cap 10; it refills every turn and unused flux is lost. **Reactor Instability:** 3 turns after reaching max flux the reactor melts down and gives unlimited flux.
- The **Summon Phase** (1 creature a turn) is now the **Materialize Phase**: as many creatures as you can pay for.
- The graveyard is now the **Waste Repository** and the banish zone is **Oblivion** ("cast into Oblivion"; cards there don't come back unless a card says so).
- Fusions use **Fusion Splices**, listed on the fusion card. No flux to fuse.
- **Adjacent:** the card in front of it on the opponent's field and the cards left and right of it on yours.
- Direct PHP damage only when your opponent has no creatures, and a destroyed defender's player takes no damage (no more excess damage carrying over).
- **Bleed:** Bleeding doesn't stack; keep the higher value. Bleed damage happens in the End Phase.
- "Once per turn" counts copies of the same card too. Players need a coin and dice (at least a d20, d10 and d6).
- Faction rules: **Goblin gold** (invest, Market Cycle: +1/+2 ATK at 5+/10+ gold, a d6 roll at 10+ gold), **Parasites** (infesting hosts), **Mutants** (the d6 Mutation and d12 Horrific Mutation tables), **Galorpian Equipment**.
- Dropped: the unfusing rule, the "max two once-per-turn effects on a fusion" rule and the deck-building limits (back in v4 and v2.1).

## Ruleset v1

The first ruleset.
- Get your opponent from **50 PHP** to 0, by attacking when they have no creatures or with card effects.
- **Deck:** exactly 30 cards; max 6 copies of a creature and 3 of a spell; a public Fusion Deck of up to 10. **Opening hand 6.** The first player doesn't draw or attack on their first turn.
- **Zones:** Creature Zone (6), Spell Zone (6), Graveyard, Banish Zone, Deck, Fusion Deck.
- **Turn:** Draw 2, Start, Main, **Summon** (1 creature), **Fusion** (1 fusion), Battle, Main 2, End.
- **Combat:** each creature attacks once per turn, even the turn it's summoned; creatures have to attack opposing creatures first; damage is simultaneous; excess damage from the last defender carries over to the player; damage is permanent. **Bleed** counters deal damage at the end of each turn, then drop by 1.
- **Fusion:** creatures on your field only, materials go under the fusion card; it takes one slot; max 2 fusions; **fusion sickness** (can't attack the turn it's made); an unfused fusion's materials come back if there's room; when a fusion dies, its materials go to the graveyard.
- **Spells:** Normal, Instant, Infinite/Field. Chains resolve last in, first out.
- Hand limit 8; can't draw → 5 damage. A fusion never grants more than two "once per turn" effects. Players need a coin for coin flips. **The card wins** if it contradicts the rules.
