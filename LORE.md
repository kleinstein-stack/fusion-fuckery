# Fusion Fuckery: World & Characters

A notepad of every creature's backstory, sometimes with a description of how they look. It's meant to paint a picture for artists and to feed the flavor text on the cards. Card rules live in the rulebook; this document is only about who these people are.

## The world

The goal is a TCG that doesn't feel generic: every creature should feel like part of the same world.

- **It's our world, just goofier and full of fantasy.** A multiversal collapse kicked off by a bunch of Reactor Meltdowns let angels, demons, goblins and stranger things loose into it.
- **It's modern.** There's gambling, stocks, memes and internet culture: brainrotted Gen Z scrolling Instagram reels. Angels use phones, demons own real estate, goblins trade penny stocks.
- **Everyone is connected.** The demon nobility is one family (the Ergof dynasty), the goblins have an old military and a new economy, and the angels have a chain of command up to the Seraph.
- **Tone:** funny, a bit crude, but every character has something real going on underneath.

## Goblins

The goblins used to be a military power, but most of their soldiers died in the Goblin Wars. Now they're becoming an economic powerhouse instead: gambling, mining, stocks and a goblin mafia.

- **Goblin:** looks like Jschlatt and is your average goblin. Enjoys the occasional gamble, acts like Mr. Money Bags and has an ego to match, with a personality similar to Jschlatt's.
- **Penny Stock Goblin:** a 1-to-1 goblin version of Jordan Belfort from *The Wolf of Wall Street*, but in his early days, when he really was trading penny stocks. He'll make it big one day, though.
- **Goblin Miner:** one of the few hardworking goblins. He spends most of his days deep in the mines doing dangerous gold mining. He has a loving wife and family, but life's hard for him.
- **Goblin Pit Boss:** a lowlife gambling addict paying off his debt to the goblin mafia by working for them as a pit boss.
- **Gob-omber:** ex goblin military and an explosives expert. The goblin military used to be a big deal in the old days, but most of them were killed in the Goblin Wars. He has PTSD and zero trigger discipline.
- **Goblin General:** ex goblin military. A very strict, typical general type with the thousand-yard stare, and a deep secret he'll tell no one. He yearns for the glory days but is slowly accepting that goblins are becoming an economic powerhouse instead of a military one.
- **Hemogoblin:** an emo version of the goblin. Still has everything that makes a goblin a goblin, but also does demonic stuff, like praying to demons for better odds.
- **Mountain Orc:** lives in the mountains with his pet goat, living a simple life off the land.

### Goblin fusions

- **Goblin King:** has insane amounts of money and loves a somewhat lavish lifestyle, buying imported Japanese cars like RX-7s and R35 Skylines. People don't think he's that lavish, because he's a goblin after all. He's democratically elected, treats everyone as an equal and is an all-round decent guy (for a goblin, that is).
- **Goblin Bombardier:** a war veteran who used to fly bombers. He once threaded the needle between two mountains while carrying an atomic bomb, still brags about it to this day and is considered a war hero.
- **Hop Goblin:** two goblins in a trench coat. Both look like Jschlatt and they share a single brain cell.
- **Scrooger** (Goblin and Blood Demon): a real mean goblin machine and the embodiment of greed. Bright red goblin skin, iced-out watches and chains, and he flaunts everything. But how? He's so greedy that surely all his money is under his mattress, so how much does he really have? (He's actually the second-richest goblin, and the fifth-richest demon after the Ergof dynasty.)

## Blood Demons and the Ergof dynasty

The underworld is run by the Ergof dynasty, a demon nobility that's rich, ancient and very dysfunctional.

- **Ergof The Dark Emperor:** emperor of the underworld. Deeply depressed and seeking vengeance for his father's death and the tragic loss of his wife. He doesn't pay attention to his daughter Salem and has never really opened up to anyone since his wife passed, but before that he was a really chill guy.
- **Salem:** daughter of Ergof The Dark Emperor, with a serious attitude because of it. Hangs out with succubi and dresses in oversized hoodies and JNCO jeans.
- **Renroc The Dark Marquis:** an ancient part of the Ergof dynasty and somehow family of Ergof. Very proud of his heritage and status.
- **Nomed The Dark Viscount:** older than Renroc, yet somehow not as powerful or as high in rank, and he's really pissed about it. Very pretentious, loves to collect human memorabilia from throughout the ages, and hosts lavish parties to make others think he's richer than he is: he actually rents the mansions he parties in.
- **Carrion The Dark Duchess:** second-richest after Ergof and also part of the Ergof dynasty. Has multiple wives and one husband, owns a large part of hell's real estate industry and mostly keeps to herself.
- **Beelzechud:** an overweight, Jabba the Hutt-looking creature indulging in any and all fast food. Has a strong opinion on "Ken-Taco-Hut" and owns a large amount of Pesi Co stock.
- **Blood Priest:** a demonic religious guy. Unlike most demons, they wear lots of colorful trinkets on their arms and ornaments in their horns, and actually care about things like nature. They're still demonic, though: they use the flesh and blood of mortals as compost.
- **Blood Imp:** an impish-looking fella (who would have thunk it) with a smoking addiction. Prefers unfiltered, tar-filled cheap cigs.
- **Vein Ripper:** a demon dog based on a St. Bernard, carrying a barrel of Jack Daniel's Honey around its neck.

### Blood Demon fusions

- **The Meat Cathedral:** a zero-brains hunk of pure lean meat. Looks like a *Doom Eternal* demon and packs a punch.
- **Beelzechad:** looks like an absolute chad.

## Angels

The angels have a chain of command: acolytes and knights at the bottom, Gabe at the top, and the Seraph above it all.

- **Cherub:** a young adult in angel years, but she's always getting into trouble because of her nuanced view of good and evil. She's really into modern human technology like phones, PCs and electronics, and knows her way around a soldering iron and an oscilloscope. Her favorite language is Holy C (just kidding, it's Python). She recently matched with Salem on Hinge.
- **Acolyte Adam:** a young acolyte of the church, trying to prove himself.
- **Sanctified Knight:** looks a lot like SwaggerSouls, rocking the same off-white varsity jacket, but with more armor, and the jacket has seen some shit. Carries a dope-ass sword sponsored by Angel Energy (use code SANCTIFIED for 20% off!). He also secretly smokes weed: even angels sin.
- **Judicator Angel:** always weighing what's morally good or evil. Carries scales, prefers a morning star as her weapon and is built like an Amazonian warrior.
- **Chapel Guard:** looks like a Vatican City guard. Not much more to say: they wield halberds.
- **Purifier Nun:** wields a book of prayers and holy water.
- **Bellkeeper Monk:** wields bell nunchucks and a staff.
- **Gabe:** the top dawg. Uses a simple dagger, but the dagger holds the souls of a thousand demons, including Ergof's father's soul.

### Angel fusions

- **Seraph of Judgment:** the OG keeper of the angel realm, who has seen it all. She doesn't concern herself with the day-to-day tasks of angels, not even at Gabe's level, and only steps in when the realm itself is threatened.
- **Seraphim Wall:** looks like a biblically accurate angel.
- **Arch Cleric:** has a lot of religious tattoos and can open his third eye.
- **Lord Lazarus** (Angel and Blood Demon): think high priest. A normal man, elevated into heaven by his holy deeds.
- **Fallen Angel** (Angel and Blood Demon): looks like a badass angel woman, but her hands and feet gradient into those of a succubus. So do her wings, from feathers to bat skin, with bat-like tips.
- **Indulgence Seller** (Angel and Goblin): has some spiritual nonsense going on, always wearing a cloak and carrying a staff. He's got what you want and what you need, but you pay with more than just gold.

## Plushies (draft)

*A starting point written by Claude so the faction fits the world. Change anything.*

- **The idea:** plush versions of everyone above, won from claw machines in arcades. The meltdowns made some of them come alive: they're still the characters they're based on, just stuffed, squeaky and very hard to keep down.
- **Look:** felt and fleece versions of the originals, with visible stitching, button eyes, tags and prize-machine price stickers. Same outfits and props as the real ones, made of fabric (Plush Vein Ripper's barrel is a felt pouch).
- **Arcade Kid:** a Gen Z kid who has sunk their entire allowance into the claw machine. "One more try. Just one more."
- **Plush Collector:** an adult collector with a basement full of mint-condition, tags-still-on plushies. Not a plushy themselves.
- **Patchwork Plush:** stitched together from the plushies that didn't make it. A bit creepy, very loyal.
- **Mega Plush:** a giant prize plush. Its owner claims he won it on the first try. Nobody believes him.
- **Claw Machine:** the arcade machine everything comes from. The prize is always just out of reach.

## Flavor text on the cards

Each card with a backstory got one short flavor line under its effect, where there was room. All of them fitted without shrinking the card text. Flavor never names real people, so references like Jschlatt or Jordan Belfort stay in this document for the artists.
