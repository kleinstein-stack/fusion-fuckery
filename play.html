Fusion Fuckery Tabletop

Two players, one screen. The table keeps track of zones, PHP, flux, gold, damage and winded creatures, works out who strikes first, and checks fusion recipes. Card effects are up to you, like at a real table: use the card menus to move cards, deal damage and change stats. Ctrl+Z undoes. Rules: see the rulebook.
